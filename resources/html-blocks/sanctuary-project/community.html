The support of animal-loving benefactors and building suppliers through our 2025-2026 Capital Campaign, including the Gala FAAN-TASTICA, is helping us realize our vision for the dogs of Cuenca.

Thanks to the generosity of animal lovers in Ecuador and around the world, 10 acres of permanent land in the Tarqui mountains have been purchased for the new SuperDogs Sanctuary. $31,000 was raised at the Gala FAAN-TASTICA toward the future home of our SuperDogs.
